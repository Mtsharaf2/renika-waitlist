Worked example

One case, traced end to end.

This is how RENiKA presents a recommendation: the inputs go in, a named rule fires, and the output arrives with the guideline version and citation attached — so it can be checked, audited and defended at the bedside.

  1. 1 Inputs

    Patient
    62-year-old with type 2 diabetes, BP 134/82 mmHg
    eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021)
    38 mL/min/1.73 m², stable across two readings > 90 days apart
    Urine ACR
    450 mg/g (≈ 51 mg/mmol)
    Current therapy
    Metformin, ramipril at maximum tolerated dose; no SGLT2 inhibitor

  2. 2 Rule

    CKD.STAGE.CGA → CKD.T2D.SGLT2I.EGFR≥20

    eGFR 30–44 → G3b. ACR > 300 mg/g → A3. Classified CKD G3b A3 (very high risk). Condition type 2 diabetes AND CKD AND eGFR ≥ 20 is satisfied and no exclusion (type 1 diabetes, kidney transplant, dialysis) applies — the SGLT2 inhibitor rule fires.

  3. 3 Recommendation

    Start an SGLT2 inhibitor (e.g. dapagliflozin 10 mg or empagliflozin 10 mg once daily) alongside the existing RAS inhibitor. Once started, continuing is reasonable even if eGFR later falls below 20, unless not tolerated or kidney replacement therapy begins.

    Strength 1 — strongEvidence A — high

  4. 4 Citation

    KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease — Recommendation 3.7.1 and Practice Point 3.7.1; CGA staging, Section 1.2. Kidney Int. 2024;105(4S):S117–S314.

    Corroborating: ESC 2023 Guidelines for the management of cardiovascular disease in patients with diabetes — SGLT2 inhibitor in T2D with CKD, Class I, Level A.

    Guideline version KDIGO CKD 2024 (April 2024)
